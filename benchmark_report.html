Deployment & Sustainability Recommendation

Based on the benchmark results, SVM Char (Character n-grams 3-5 LinearSVC) is highly recommended for applications prioritizing accuracy, reaching a top F1-Score of 0.9905. However, it requires a larger model disk footprint (8.63 MB), has higher inference latency (2.75 ms), and incurs a carbon footprint of 10.3142 mg CO2eq per benchmark evaluation.

If you are running in a resource-constrained environment (edge/mobile devices or high-concurrency low-latency webhooks) or prioritize environmental sustainability (Green AI), HistGradientBoosting is the best alternative. It achieves a blazing fast 8.19 ms single inference latency, 122.1 classifications per second, a minimal model footprint of 0.32 MB, and a carbon footprint of only 8.4840 mg CO2eq.
